Per-coin source picker

Choose the exchange behind every coin, or let Auto pick the best source and fail over when one is down.

⚡

Live where it matters

Realtime prices on your watchlist (up to 20 pairs across different exchanges) and widgets — nothing else.

🔋

Battery-smart

In Save mode sockets close in background and reopen on return. Realtime mode streams ~1s updates via a foreground service.

📊

Pro charts

Candles, line or area. 13 timeframes, MA/EMA/Bollinger, RSI and MACD sub-charts, OHLC crosshair, pinch-zoom.

🔔

Price alerts

Above/below price or ±% 24h, single or repeatable, with quick-action notifications that deep-link into the chart.

🔒

Private by design

No account, no backend, no analytics. Optional biometric lock and JSON backup/restore. GPL-3.0 open source.
